## Core idea

Most knowledge systems store the current truth and overwrite it when things change. This domain explores systems that store the **full history of understanding**: when a fact was true in the world, when the system came to believe it, and every human decision made along the way. The result is a knowledge base that can answer not just *"what is true now?"* but *"what did we know, when did we know it, and why did we believe it?"*
